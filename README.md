# Supply-Chain-Analysis
Supply Chain Analysis using SQL, Python, and Power BI



## Project Overview

This project analyzes supply chain operations using SQL, Python, and Power BI to uncover insights related to revenue, supplier performance, inventory management, shipping costs, and product quality.

The objective was to identify inefficiencies and provide actionable recommendations that improve supply chain performance and profitability.

---

## Business Problem

Supply chain organizations generate large volumes of data related to suppliers, inventory, products, logistics, and quality control.

Without proper analysis, businesses struggle to identify:

* High-performing product categories
* Supplier delays
* Rising shipping costs
* Inventory shortages
* Quality issues
* Revenue growth opportunities

---

## Key Insights

* Cosmetics generated the highest revenue.
* Haircare showed the lowest sales performance.
* Supplier 3 had the fastest lead time.
* Supplier 1 had the lowest defect rate.
* Multiple SKUs were below safe stock levels.
* Pending inspections were highest.
* No strong correlation between price and revenue.

---

## Business Recommendations

* Increase focus on high-performing product categories.
* Improve promotion for low-performing products.
* Replenish low-stock inventory.
* Strengthen supplier partnerships.
* Improve quality control processes.
* Monitor KPIs through dashboards.
